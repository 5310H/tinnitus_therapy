Decorrelated Noise

Independent noise streams per ear reduce pattern-matching, promoting habituation.

Clinical Significance

Based on research by Tass et al. (2012), decorrelated noise disrupts pathological neural synchrony by providing independent, non-correlated signals to each ear to promote neural desynchronization.

Hearing Compensation (EQ) ○

Boost frequencies where you have hearing loss to reduce neural strain. Boost carefully.
