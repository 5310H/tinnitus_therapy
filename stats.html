Progress & Statistics

Track your Tinnitus Handicap Inventory (THI) scores over time.

Loudness Growth Trend

Objective volume (%) at which sound is perceived as "Loud". Lower values may indicate hyperacusis.

TMC Q-factor Trend

Higher Q-factor indicates a sharper auditory filter around your tinnitus frequency.

Minimum Masking Level (MML) Trend

Objective volume (%) required to mask your tinnitus. A downward trend can indicate lower subjective volume.

Suppression Trend (RI)

Higher values indicate longer periods of tinnitus suppression after sound exposure.

THI Score Trend

Lower scores indicate less tinnitus-related distress.
