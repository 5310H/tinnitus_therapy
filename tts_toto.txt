--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STATUS & PRIORITY LEGEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status Flags:
  [ ]  Todo        - Task is queued and not yet started.
  [/]  In Progress - Task is actively being worked on.
  [X]  Completed   - Task is fully implemented, verified, and released.
  [!]  Blocked     - Task is on hold due to external dependencies or blockers.
  [-]  Deferred    - Task is postponed for a future milestone.

Priority Scale:
  1 - Critical     - Blocking release, clinical safety issue, or core DSP bug.
  2 - High         - Important feature, core UI issue, or major usability need.
  3 - Medium       - Standard feature, minor bug fix, or general improvement.
  4 - Low          - Nice-to-have enhancement, refactoring, or style polish.
  5 - Planning     - Long-term research, experimental design, or idea phase.

Versioning Scheme:
  Follows the date-based scheme matching package.json and whats_new.json 
  (e.g., 2026.06.33, 2026.07.01).
