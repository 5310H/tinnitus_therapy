What's New in v

Recent improvements to your therapy experience:

Latest (v2.1.0) - May 2026

  • AI Refinements: Enhanced chat interface with typing indicators and improved context for Pattern Finder analysis.
  • Wellness Tools: Integrated AI-generated mindfulness scripts into the Thought Record workflow to assist in grounding.
  • Dashboard Performance: Optimized loading of AI insights and improved real-time connectivity validation for Gemini keys.

v2.0.0: Major AI Integration - May 2026

  • Clinical Pilot Program: New inquiry system for professional partners.
  • AI-Powered Features (Google Gemini): Integrated 5 new AI-driven tools for crisis support, personalized soundscapes, pattern analysis, TRT education, and clinical report summaries.

v1.5.0: Professional Integration - May 2026

  • Dual-Stimulus (Bimodal) Support: Synchronize auditory bursts with wireless tactile pulses using the new Finger Pacer hardware or mobile haptics.
  • Clinical Summary for Doctors: Generate professional technical PDFs and handouts summarizing protocols and citations to share with audiologists.
  • Community Sharing: Use the "Share Setup" tool to copy settings formatted for forums like Tinnitus Talk and Reddit.
  • Breathing Wave Pacer: Integrated visual and auditory pacing for resonance breathing during therapeutic sessions.

v1.4.0: Interface & Guidance - April 2026

  • Interactive Tutorials: Voice-narrated UI walkthroughs and visual highlights for every therapy and tool.
  • Guided Onboarding: A visual "Path to Relief" sequence on the dashboard to help you complete your clinical setup.
  • PMR Support: Guided relaxation sequence in CBT & Wellness with auditory cues to help manage distress.
  • Better Organization: Dashboard tools are now split into "Support Tools" and "System Utilities" for more intuitive navigation.
  • Performance & Safety: Improved DSP validation and new options for data backup or factory reset.
